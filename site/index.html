Car Projects

Everything running under pcarbuddy, and where to find it.

Market trackers, auction research, inventory catalogues, two iPhone apps, and the scrapers that feed them. Fifteen projects that all turned out to be about the same question: what is this car actually worth, and what will it cost me later?

Live on the web

  • Porsche Tracker Private

    Tracks the US Porsche market — every new, CPO and used listing — daily, and analyses it by segment: what a given car is worth, how long it sits, and which way prices are actually moving.

    porsche-tracker.fulgent.org · ~/Projects/porsche-tracker
  • Porsche P.I. Live

    A private investigator for Porsche models. Hand it a Bring a Trailer model page and it pulls the complete auction record, reads the recent listings and their comment threads with Claude, and returns a case file: pricing analytics, a dossier of known failure points, and browsable comparables. Case files are public; running an investigation needs the password.

    porsche-pi.fulgent.org · ~/Projects/porsche-pi
  • Auction PI Live

    The same idea pointed at every marque. Paste a listing and it reads every completed sale for that model, works through the recent ones line by line, and returns a fair-value estimate, the failure points that model keeps showing, and a profile of the seller built from their own public record.

    auction-pi-production.up.railway.app · ~/Projects/auction-pi
  • AuctionWatch Live

    One search box across eleven listing sites at once — Cars & Bids, Bring a Trailer, Hagerty, PCar Market and eBay Motors on the auction side; Craigslist, Cars.com, Porsche Finder, CarMax, Carvana and Hemmings on the fixed-price side.

    auctionwatch-production.up.railway.app · ~/Projects/auctionwatch
  • Caddy Buddy Live

    A national catalogue of the two Cadillac Blackwings — and, kept deliberately apart from them, the CT5-V and CT4-V that share their bodies. Built from cadillac.com's own inventory API plus the dealers' sites, then enriched with NHTSA vPIC VIN decodes.

    caddy-buddy.fulgent.org · ~/Projects/caddy-buddy
  • Monroney Buddy API

    Window stickers as a service. Storage, parsing and one API across every OEM, so the scrapers stop each parsing Monroney labels their own way and keeping their own copies. Caddy Buddy reads from it and pushes back what it catches. Token required, so the bare URL answers 401 by design.

    monroneybuddy.com

On your phone

  • Garage Buddy App Store

    Mileage, fuel and running costs for every car you own. Photograph the odometer and it reads the number off it; the data lives on your iPhone or in an ordinary spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. Ships as Garage Buddy: Car Tracker.

    garage-buddy.app · ~/Projects/garage-buddy, ~/Projects/garage-buddy-site
  • Rally Buddy TestFlight

    A co-driver for everyday roads. Mark passing lanes, residential zones and tight corners on roads you drive, and it calls them out before you reach them, the way a rally co-driver reads pace notes. Everything stays on the device.

    testflight.apple.com/join/Yfgj5x49 · ~/Projects/rally-buddy

Not deployed

Local repos — the data layer under the sites above, plus the earlier attempts they grew out of.

  • Porsche Finder

    Aggregates US Porsche inventory from finder.porsche.com and independent dealer sites, downloads and parses the window stickers and build sheets for MSRP and every factory option, and pulls Carfax and days-on-market. The scraper and the web viewer are deliberately separate, with SQLite between them.

    ~/Projects/finder-scraper
  • GR Tracker

    The same machinery as Porsche Finder, pointed at Toyota's GR line — GR Supra, GR86, GR Corolla — and kept as a separate single-make instance. There is no Toyota equivalent of Porsche's Monroney endpoint, so it carries no window stickers.

    ~/Projects/supra-scraper
  • CarMap

    Parses a CARFAX report PDF in the browser and plots the car's whole history on a map — ownership changes, service visits, accidents, sales and inspections, geocoded and joined by the car's travel path. No server, and the PDF is never uploaded.

    ~/Projects/carmap
  • CarTracker

    An earlier SwiftUI take on what became Garage Buddy: multi-car mileage and expenses with Google Sheets as the backend and OCR odometer scanning.

    ~/Projects/cartrack
  • pcar

    The original Porsche dealer scraper — a Perl script that Vercel's bot check kept blocking, rewritten in Python and Playwright to get a headless browser past the JavaScript challenge. SQLite out the back.

    ~/Projects/pcar
  • bidgraph

    A Python project with a SQLite store and a test suite, and no README yet to say what it is.

    ~/Projects/bidgraph
